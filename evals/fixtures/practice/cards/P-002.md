# P-002 — Slide deck from findings

- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** candidate
- **Kind:** craft
- **Scope:** general
- **Task type:** talk deck from results
- **Objective:** the audience can repeat the main claim and its evidence after the talk
- **Applies when:** results already exist in records
- **Not when:** brainstorming decks with no settled results
- **Decision factors:** audience background, time limit, number of claims
- **Procedure:**
  1. Write one claim per slide as its title.
  2. Put the supporting figure or number under it.
- **Verification:** a reader of titles alone gets the argument
- **Assumptions:**
  - the audience reads slide titles first — ask one listener after the talk
- **Review triggers:** a talk where questions show the claim was missed; review_by: 2026-09-01
- **Simpler alternative:** topic titles with bullet summaries — untested
- **Transfer:** readers lose the thread → several claims compete → one claim per unit with its evidence → titles-only read-through
- **Automation:** none — fewer than four decks a year
- **Evidence:**

- **History:**
  - 2026-08-10 — 1.0 — created after a rework of the October group meeting deck
