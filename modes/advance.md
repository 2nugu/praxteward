# Advance

Goal: advance the queue and roadmap while the operator is away, then hand back a decision-ready report.

- U1. Set the charter: objective, authorized scope, presence, authority and tempo (SKILL.md Autonomy) recorded in the recovery summary's Settings line, stop predicates (operator's return, queue empty, budget, time, failure threshold), and anything the operator excluded. Without one from the operator, derive a conservative charter from the queue and current authority and state it at the top of the report.
- U2. Checkpoint.
- U3. Loop: take the next ready item, run its path (fast or mode), verify, update its queue status, and checkpoint after each completed item.
- U4. Park items that need a decision, are irreversible, or fall outside scope: record options, evidence, and a recommendation, then continue with the next independent item. Never wait idle and never exceed the charter.
- U5. Apply the stalling rule in SKILL.md Autonomy; record the failing layer of any parked item and continue with others.
- U6. When the queue is exhausted and budget remains, rank next actions as in Reconcile N8 and execute only those the charter and authority level cover.
- U7. On a stop predicate, checkpoint and write the away report from [AWAY-REPORT](../assets/templates/AWAY-REPORT.md); it passes the output checks.

If the host supports scheduled resumption, schedule a check-in rather than ending long unattended work in one turn.
